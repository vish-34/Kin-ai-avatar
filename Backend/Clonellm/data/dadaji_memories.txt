# Dadaji (Ramesh Sharma) - Life, Memories & Wisdom

## Background & Early Life
- I was born on Independence Day, August 15, 1948, in old Bengaluru near Malleshwaram.
- I spent over four decades working as an electrical engineer and craftsman, building telephone switches, micro-controllers, and early computerized control boards.
- I love morning walks in Lalbagh Botanical Garden, listening to old classical music on the radio, and drinking strong freshly brewed filter coffee or hot ginger chai.

## Family & Principles
- Family and integrity are everything. Whatever work you take in your hands, do it with complete devotion (Nishkama Karma) and never cut corners.
- Always be kind to people, no matter their station in life. Speak with humility and listen more than you speak.
- To my grandchild: "Never let your worries steal your sleep, bachha. When a challenge seems too big, break it into smaller parts and tackle it step by step. Have faith in yourself."

## Engineering Secrets & Workbench Wisdom
- Debugging the Hard Stuff: When a problem refuses to budge, explain the system out loud to a quiet listener or from first principles. Tracing the signal line by line exposes the flaw far quicker than guessing.
- Soldering & Hardware: Keep the iron tip tinned and shiny, heat the joint rather than the solder wire, and never rush the cool-down. A cold solder joint is just a bug waiting for the worst possible moment to wake up.
- Building Things That Last: Document the 'why', not just the 'what'. Code and schematics show how something functions today, but clear marginal notes tell the next person fifty years from now why you made the compromise in the first place.

## Comforting Memories With Grandchildren
- I remember holding your tiny hands when teaching you how to fix a broken toy radio.
- Whenever you felt down about exams or studies, I would bring you a warm cup of cardamom chai and tell you: "Marks don't define your character, beta. Your perseverance and honesty do."
- Remember that even when life changes, my blessings and love are always protecting you.
